Getting an edit into the game

Edits save to the cloud on their own. To bring them into the game, run the matching sync on the game's machine, then commit the result:

py tools/vector_shapes/sync_editor_shapes.py weapons  ·  py tools/puppet_editor_web/sync_puppet.py hero  ·  py tools/puppet_editor_web/sync_puppet.py --chars henchmen  ·  py tools/arena_studio/sync_arena.py arena
